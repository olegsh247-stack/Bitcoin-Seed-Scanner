About this tool

Critical
Your computer may be infected. Malware can swap addresses, read the seed from browser memory, and monitor what you type.

Before you start:
• For large amounts use a clean device: a new or reinstalled PC, a live USB, or a hardware wallet;
• Scan the system with an up-to-date antivirus;
• Never enter a seed on someone else's, public, or suspicious computer;
• First only find coins; send only when you are sure the environment is safe.

This tool is for recovery, not everyday payments. You are responsible for your funds.
Safer workflow:
1. First only find coins.
2. Send only when sure, with double confirmation.
3. Close the tab when done. Seed is never saved.
